| `ielts-academic-format` | academic, format | IELTS Academic has Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking. | `ielts-writing-criteria` | writing, assessment | Writing assessment uses Task Achievement/Task Response, Coherence and Cohesion, Lexical Resource, and Grammatical Range and Accuracy. The tasks are assessed independently and the criteria are equally weighted within each task. | https://ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/writing-test-resources |
| `ielts-academic-reading-format` | reading, academic | Academic Reading is 60 minutes, has three sections, 40 questions and a 1–9 whole/half-band score. The page lists question types including multiple choice, T/F/NG, matching, completion and short answer. | https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-reading |
| `ielts-scoring` | scoring, listening, reading, writing, speaking | Overall band is the average of the four component scores, rounded according to the published .25/.75 rule. The page provides section-score guidance and the four Writing and four Speaking criteria. | https://ielts.org/take-a-test/your-results/ielts-scoring-in-detail |
| `ielts-writing-band-descriptors` | writing, assessment | Official Writing Task 1 and Task 2 band descriptors. Use the original PDF for detailed assessment rather than a simplified paraphrase. | https://ielts.org/cdn/Guides/ielts-writing-band-descriptors.pdf |

## Query routing

- **Format or timing question:** retrieve the specific test-format source first.
- **Band conversion question:** retrieve `ielts-scoring`; state that precise raw-score thresholds can vary by test version where the official page says so.
- **Writing or Speaking evaluation:** retrieve the official descriptor PDF and relevant format/criteria page; do not promise an official score.
- **Preparation advice:** label as teaching guidance unless the exact claim comes from a listed official source.
- **Registration, price, availability, policy or current date-specific rules:** do not answer from this static register; retrieve a current official local test-provider or IELTS page.

## Exclusions

This public corpus deliberately does not contain private IELTSSHOKH offers, prices, schedules, student information, internal channel strategy, or unpublished teaching material. Those require owner-approved internal sources or a later authorized connector.
