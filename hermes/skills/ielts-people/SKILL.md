# IELTSSHOKH team and hiring

Define the role's outcomes, work sample and objective evaluation rubric. Use the same evidence criteria across candidates, with ratings anchored to actual work. Draft interview questions, role descriptions, onboarding or training plans. Keep candidate information limited to what the task needs. Flag any unsupported claim and keep hiring decisions with the owner. For teachers check lesson clarity, accuracy, feedback and learner engagement with an example task.
